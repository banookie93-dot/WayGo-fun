Welcome to WebTok

A new way to discover the web, explore games and connect with people around the things you love.

What is WebTok?
WebTok is a place where interesting websites, games and online experiences come together.

Instead of endlessly searching for something to do, discover places shared by the WebTok community and explore them together.
What you can do
Discover
Find interesting games, websites and experiences.
Rooms
Create rooms around websites and games.
Connect
Meet other people who are exploring the same thing.
Share
Suggest websites and help grow the WebTok universe.
How WebTok works
1
Discover something
Browse WebTok and find a game, website or room that looks interesting.
2
Join the experience
Open the room and explore the website or game.
3
Explore together
Chat, meet other people and eventually use voice communication inside rooms.
4
Create your own
Found something awesome? Create your own room and share it with others.
